## What is the Divide & Conquer Technique

The Divide & Conquer technique described as being implemented recusively to
breakdown problems into smaller subproblems. Each subproblem is solved
individually, then those results are combined to find solve the original
problem. As I work though this topic I'm asked to consider two questions:

- What is a divide-and-conquer strategy?
- How do you approach and solve a problme using divide-and-conquer strategy.

We have touched up on the first question, but as learn more about
divide-and-conquer we will begin to build an intuition for this topic, at least
thats the goal.

## Divide & Conquer Process

There are three steps to this technique:

1. Divide - break the problme into smaller instances of the original problem

2. Conquer - Solve the subproblems recursively independently of one another

3. Combine - Comebine the solutions of the subproblems to get the solution for
   the larger problem
